Core System Features

Engineered for absolute municipal transparency, accountability, and time-bound delivery.
Feature 01

1. Easy Complaint Submission

Simple 1-minute grievance registration with citizen details, location, and clear issue description. Receives a permanent unique tracking ID.

Feature 02

2. Department Auto-Assignment

No manual department guesswork. The backend service layer intelligently auto-routes complaints straight to the responsible municipal department.

Feature 03

3. Real-Time Complaint Tracking

Visual step-by-step timeline shows current progress from submission to investigation, field work, and final closure.

Feature 04

4. SLA Monitoring

Every civic category is bound by fixed statutory resolution days stored in the database. Calculated deadlines are tracked continuously.

Feature 05

5. Automatic Escalation

Automated background scheduler flags overdue complaints and escalates them to Senior Zonal Commissioners with audit timestamps.

Feature 06

6. Resolution Rating

Citizens provide 1 to 5 star ratings and qualitative feedback once resolved, holding departments accountable for work quality.

Help & Municipal SLA Citizen Charter

Understanding your rights, resolution timelines, and grievance escalation.

🕒 Statutory SLA Rules

Every civic grievance has a legally binding SLA based on severity. The timer starts automatically upon submission. If work is not completed prior to the deadline, the system automatically triggers an escalation record.

⚡ Automatic Escalation

Complaints exceeding SLA are reassigned directly to Senior Zonal Commissioners. The system prevents duplicate escalations while recording the exact reason and timestamp for audit scrutiny.

⭐ Citizen Rating Rights

Once marked as RESOLVED, citizens can submit an unalterable 1 to 5 star rating with feedback. Ratings hold municipal field units accountable for long-term road and water quality.
